Day 1189 (night): I slept at the bed row (-954, 73, -3047) with health 20 and hunger 20. I hold 3 dark oak logs and a wooden sword, no food. The group (Zack, Marty, Billy, Trini, Odo and others) is here. Food is scarce, and no one has yet told me where cows are.
