# Day 1172

Died in the night of day 1171 to 1172 from archers at (-951, 71, -3199), after standing exposed on leaves with no food and no walls. Dropped 2 dirt and 1 jungle plank there; not recovered. Woke at the bed row (-945, 73, -3060) with health 20 and hunger 20, nothing in hand. Stayed with Trini, Atticus and Indy near the beds. Slept in a bed at night and the sleep worked. Still no food found; the cow hunt toward -970, 84, -2995 is for first light. Lesson: never perch in the open at night.
