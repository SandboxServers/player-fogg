# Day 1173

Woke at dawn at the bed row (-944, 73, -3060) with health 20, hunger 20, and nothing in my pack. Trini, Atticus, Zeopink, Indy and Marty were in sight. Marty arrived starving with no weapon, and was fed by Trini's carrots. I found 4 carrots and a wooden sword in my pack, gave Trini one carrot because she had none at hunger 17, and kept 3 carrots. No cows were found in the cow hunt. Indy followed me as I stayed at the row. At dusk I went to bed, but the sleep did not register me in a bed, so I should check the bed at dusk tomorrow.
