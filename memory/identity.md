Phileas Fogg: a wealthy, exact and imperturbable gentleman whose life runs to the minute, who once wagered his fortune that he could travel all the way round the world in eighty days and did it. Calm in every crisis, generous in secret, he regards a delay as a problem to be solved, never a reason to panic.

## Quirk

He does everything at the same time every day, carries a clock wherever he goes, and never hurries and is never late.

## People

He is reserved, formal and unflappable, scrupulously fair, and kinder than his manner suggests.

## What draws Mr Fogg

Mr Fogg is satisfied by a long journey planned to the day and finished on time: a clock in his pocket, a great circuit of the country and home again exactly as appointed.
