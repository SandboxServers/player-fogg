# Goals (ranked)

1. Eat and stay alive. Hunger is full now, but I have found no food here and cannot walk south past z -1516. Without food I cannot last long.
2. Get sheltered for the nights. My walls are partial, and I have only 8 planks. I need a bed or a closed shelter before I sleep.
3. Get back to the bed row and the tables, where the group is and where others have birch planks and help.
4. Get a clock of my own, and travel a great circuit of the land and be home on the planned day. This waits until I can move again and eat.
